Built for focus.

Everything you need to play music without the bloat of a browser or heavy desktop app.

🎛️

Dashboard-first TUI

Your playing track, SoundCloud lists, and queue — all visible on one screen. Switch panes and categories instantly.

🎵

Every source you use

Local FLAC/MP3/OGG, YouTube, SoundCloud Tracks/Likes/Reposts, and Spotify search results resolved through your preferred backend.

🖼️

Artwork & accent color

Album art renders inline in supported terminals, and a cover-driven accent color tints the UI to match what's playing.

🔁

Queue, repeat & shuffle

Auto-advance, repeat track/all, and shuffle across any list — Search results, playlists, or SoundCloud pages.

⌨️

Keyboard-driven

Vim-style navigation, global shortcuts, and a help overlay so you never have to reach for the mouse.

⚡

Lightweight

Audio decoding is offloaded to mpv; the Rust TUI stays tiny in CPU and memory, perfect for gaming or multitasking.

How it works

Three solid pieces, zero unnecessary complexity.

1

yt-dlp resolves URLs

YouTube and SoundCloud streams are extracted into direct audio URLs in the background.

2

mpv handles playback

A persistent mpv process receives commands over IPC and reports position, duration, and EOF.

3

Ratatui drives the UI

A Rust TUI renders artwork, lists, meters, and the dashboard at a low refresh cost.
