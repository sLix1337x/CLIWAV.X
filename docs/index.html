Why not just use a browser? Browsers are heavy. CLIWAV.X uses a native Rust TUI and offloads audio decoding to mpv, so the CPU and memory footprint stays tiny — built to stay out of the way while you're gaming or working.

Features

Built for focus.

Everything you need to play music, without the bloat of a browser tab or a heavy desktop app.

Dashboard-first TUI

Now playing, your SoundCloud Tracks/Likes/Reposts, and the queue — all visible at once. No username configured? Genre buckets like Trending and Lo-Fi work out of the box.

Every source you use

Local FLAC/MP3/OGG/OPUS/M4A/WAV, YouTube and SoundCloud via yt-dlp, and Spotify search resolved through YouTube or your own library.

Inline album artwork

Artwork renders directly in the terminal via ratatui-image — full color in Kitty, iTerm2, or WezTerm, a crisp halfblock fallback everywhere else.

Cover-driven accent color

A vibrant color is pulled from each track's artwork and tints the Now Playing panel and volume meter. Press t to switch to a curated palette instead.

Unified queue & shuffle

Mix tracks from any source in one queue. Repeat off/track/all, shuffle, and auto-advance through whatever list you're playing from.

Keyboard-driven

Vim-style navigation, global shortcuts for every action, and an in-app help overlay — the mouse never needs to leave the desk.

Architecture

How it works

Four solid pieces, zero unnecessary complexity.

1

yt-dlp resolves URLs

YouTube and SoundCloud streams are extracted into direct audio URLs in the background.

2

mpv handles playback

A persistent mpv process receives commands over IPC and reports position, duration, and EOF.

3

SQLite indexes your library

Local metadata is read with lofty and indexed in SQLite for instant search.

4

Ratatui drives the UI

A Rust TUI renders artwork, lists, and meters at a low refresh cost — even in game mode.
